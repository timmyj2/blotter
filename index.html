How to

Dump first. Sort later.

  1. Dump is the note. Bold, underline, and bullets work in the box. Attach a project if it belongs to one. Click a recent note to edit it here. Ctrl+Enter saves.
  2. Right-click a tag or project to delete it. Deleting a project keeps the notes.
  3. Loose is the unsorted pile. Pick a note and give it a project, a date, or both. A note sitting a week gets called out on Now.
  4. Organize loose notes groups notes that share words, a name, a #tag, or a date. Undo the last sort puts them back.
  5. Now leads with the one thing to do next, then the rest of the week. Calendar shows which days have something on them. Click a note to edit its text, project, or due date.
  6. Projects opens one window per project, as many as you want. The year bar shows where those projects overlap.
  7. Map draws the links. Type [[a name]] in a note to connect it to a matching title. Search takes words, "a phrase", #tag, or [[name]], and lists what that note connects to.
  8. Your notes stay on this Google account. Another person on the list cannot open them.
