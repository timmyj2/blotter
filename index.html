How to

Dump first. Sort later.

  1. Dump is where a thought goes. Keep it with no date, or press Due today so it shows up on Now. You can also drop a .md or .txt file.
  2. Loose is the unsorted pile. Pick a note and give it a project, a date, or both. A note sitting a week gets called out on Now.
  3. Organize loose notes groups notes that share words, a name, a #tag, or a date. Undo the last sort puts them back.
  4. Now leads with the one thing to do next, then the rest of the week. Calendar shows which days have something on them.
  5. Map draws the links. Type [[a name]] in a note to connect it to a matching title. Search takes words, "a phrase", #tag, or [[name]], and lists what that note connects to.
  6. Your notes stay on this Google account. Another person on the list cannot open them.
